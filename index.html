A few things to know

Do I need an API key?

No paid API key is required for this preview. Explained uses the DeepSeek website in your browser. You may need to sign in, and DeepSeek’s own usage limits apply.

What gets shared?

With lookups enabled, your selected phrase and optionally up to 600 nearby characters are sent to DeepSeek using your website login. You can inspect the context, turn it off, and pause lookups. DeepSeek may retain and use chats under its policy and your account settings.

Where does it work?

Normal HTML reading pages in Chrome 114 or later. PDFs, browser settings pages and document editors are outside this preview’s scope.

Explained is an experimental Chrome extension. Download it, load it in Chrome, and give your next unfamiliar phrase a little attention.

  2. 02Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
